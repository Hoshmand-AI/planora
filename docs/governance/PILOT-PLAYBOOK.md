# Paid pilot playbook (30/60/90 days)

**Who:** project-controls teams at general contractors or owners, with 2–5 active projects and P6 or MS Project in use.

**Positioning:** Planora is the intelligence, quality and recovery layer around the scheduler's existing tool. Schedules are imported, checked and improved, then exported back to P6 or MS Project. It does not replace them.

| Phase | Activities | Success measures |
|---|---|---|
| Days 0–30 | Upload current schedules; run DCMA and data questions; build one new schedule with the interview | Time to first analysis under 30 min; at least 3 issues found per schedule that the team agrees are real |
| Days 31–60 | Monthly updates through Planora; recovery options on a late project; Basis of Schedule for one submittal | Review time reduced ≥ 30% versus baseline; recovery option adopted or discussed with the owner |
| Days 61–90 | Firm history from 3+ completed projects; backtest; executive readout | Forecast error vs actual measured; decision to convert |

**Commercials:** a fixed pilot fee, credited toward the first year if they convert. Written success criteria are signed at kickoff.

**Evidence collected:** before/after metrics, quotes, a reference permission, and a case study.
