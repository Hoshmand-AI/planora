# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@hoshmand.ai** with a description, the steps to reproduce and the impact. Please don't open a public issue for security problems.

- We acknowledge reports within **2 business days** and give a first assessment within **5 business days**.
- We fix confirmed issues within the time limits in [docs/security/VULNERABILITY-MANAGEMENT.md](docs/security/VULNERABILITY-MANAGEMENT.md) (critical: 7 days) and tell you when the fix ships.
- We won't take legal action against good-faith research that avoids privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption, and that stays within your own test accounts.

## Supported versions

Only the current production release (the `main` branch, deployed continuously) receives security fixes.

## How Planora is secured

See [docs/security/SECURITY-OVERVIEW.md](docs/security/SECURITY-OVERVIEW.md) for the architecture and controls, and [docs/security/CONTROL-MATRIX.md](docs/security/CONTROL-MATRIX.md) for the mapping to OWASP ASVS, NIST CSF 2.0 and NIST SP 800-171.
